# Post-processing Workflow:

After the analysis what should happen is:
A. there are three markdown files per page. But for certain pages that are pretty clear or not high-value it's possible that the deep analysis document is not generated. 

 1. OCR Text Transcription [md file] - this is more of a basic OCR document that only lists the text that was transcribed from the document. It does not try to analyze images or seals or signatures. 
 2. Full AI Transcription (Including Images) [md file]: this is all the text and images on the document are tagged or described in this document.
 3. Basic Analysis [json file]: That provides; document title + page x of x, a short description, fraud score, quality score, formatting score, optional notes.
 4. Deep Analysis [json file] : This scan results in a deep report that may send her around a specific signature or image or initial or logo or clause of text. Whatever was flagged. The deep analysis could include all three of the following processes:
    
	 1. does a deep internal analysis and then 
	 2. compares with external database (landing.ai for example) and does 
	 3. online research to try and determine if this document is a fraud and what may be missing from it. 
	    ( this is only generated if the page has been flagged by the "Basic Analysis" scan or if it is part of the workflow due to it, sensitive or critical nature.)
